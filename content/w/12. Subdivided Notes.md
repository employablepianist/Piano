When we want to play more than one note per beat, we *subdivide* the beat. When playing double a quarter note's speed we play an *Eighth Note*. When quadrupling a quarter note we play a *Sixteenth Note*.

We can count Eighth Notes beat in two ways:

1. Counting the "ands" between the numbers.
	a. 1-and 2-and 3-and 4-and
2. Using syllables to vocalize the rhythm
	a. Taki Taki Taki Taki, or;
	b. Takadimi Takadimi, or;
	c. Takadimi Takijono

We can count Sixteenth Notes beat in two ways:
1. Counting the "ands" between the numbers.
	a. 1-e-and-a 2-e-and-a 3-e-and-a 4-e-and-a
2. Using syllables to vocalize the rhythm
	a. Takadimi Takadimi Takadimi Takadimi, or;
	b. Takadimi Takajonu, etc.
