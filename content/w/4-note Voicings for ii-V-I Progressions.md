>  Workflow
>
> 1. Find a basic, unsubstituted closed voicing.
> 2. Substitute notes from categories R and 5 to taste.
> 3. Add drops or other octave displacements to the voicing.

# Step 1 - Find a basic, unsubstituted closed voicing.

There are only four possible (unsubstituted) closed voicing pathways for a ii-V-I. Here they are:
<br/>

## Pathway 1
Top voice plays R-5-R.
