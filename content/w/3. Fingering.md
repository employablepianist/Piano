At the piano we communicate using finger numbers. When we want to play a specific note with a specific finger we will use a number to express that.

In both hands, the following numbers are assigned to fingers:

|  **Finger**   | **Number** |
| :-----------: | :--------: |
|     Thumb     |     1      |
| Index finger  |     2      |
| Middle finger |     3      |
|  Ring finger  |     4      |
| Pinky finger  |     5      |

Note, this means that your left thumb is left hand 1, and your right thumb is right hand 1. The finger numbers do NOT reverse when changing hands. Thumb is always 1, 5 is always pinky.

> Exercises:
>
> Play every C on the keyboard with each finger. Speak the finger number aloud when playing each C.
>
> Repeat the exercise with other keys as well.
>
>When playing a single note, make sure idle fingers are relaxing on the keytops, and your hand is resembling the shape outlined in Concept 1.
