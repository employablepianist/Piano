# Concept - Dotted Notes
## Adding Value to a Note

When we add a dot to a note, we increase that note's value by half of it's original length.

| Note         | Original amount of beats | Amount of Beats added by a dot | **Total amount of beats when dotted** |
| ------------ | ------------------------ | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Whole note   | 4                        | 2                              | 6                                     |
| Half note    | 2                        | 1                              | 3                                     |
| Quarter note | 1                        | .5                             | 1.5                                   |
| Eighth note  | .5                       | .25                            | .75                                   |
<br/>

Technically, every dotted note could be written as a tied note. Most of the time the composer just wants something a little cleaner looking, and a dot is a great short hand.
