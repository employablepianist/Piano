+++## Visually Mapping the Piano

The piano is divided up visually by alternating sets of black keys. Notice that the black keys alternate between groups of twos and threes.
<br/>

![Empty keyboard](/Piano/images/Empty_keyboard.svg)

## Finding C and F

To the direct left of every group of two black keys is the note “C”. This diagram directs you to play C with finger 1, the thumb. However, play the note with all fingers and with both hands.

![1 on C](/Piano/images/1_on_C.svg)

To the direct left of every group of three black keys is the note “F”. Again, this diagram directs you to play C with finger 1, the thumb. However, play the note with all fingers and with both hands.

![1 on F](/Piano/images/1_on_F.svg)

> Exercises:
> 
> 1. Select a single note and play it in every octave of the keyboard. Use both hands and experiment with moving from note to note.
> 
> 2. Experiment with playing two notes at once and taking stock of their names.
> 
> 3. Experiment with playing with both hands, each hand pressing down a note. Feel the hands come down together.
