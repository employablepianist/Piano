# Lesson - Finding Notes on the Staff

The music staff is composed of five lines and four spaces. The right hand and the left hand read notes differently. They do this because the clefs designate the way we interpret the lines and spaces.
<br/>
Remember, for now, the top staff reads what the right hand plays, the bottom staff reads what the left hand plays.

## C to Shining C
<br/>

Another helpful trick to remember when reading the staff is that the Cs mirror each other visually across the treble and bass clefs. For instance, high C is the third space *up* the treble clef, and the third space *down* the bass clef.
