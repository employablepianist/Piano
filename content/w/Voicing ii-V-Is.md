Notation:

(LH) (RH)

Pattern A:
	
	ii - (R 7) (3 5 R)
	V - (R 3) (7 R 5)
	I - (R 7/6) (3 5 R)

Pattern B:
	
	ii - (R 3) (7 1 5)
	V - (R 7) (3 5 1)
	I - (R 3) (7/6 R 5)

# 1 7 and 1 3 shells become SATB

You may have noticed in the left hand we'll often be playing 1 7 and 1 3 shells. We can convert these voicings into rootless voicings by taking the lowest note of the right hand and putting it into the left hand.

Note that this will create a system wherein left hand is playing 3s and 7s, and right hand is playing other chord tones.

Examples:

Rootless Pattern A:

	ii - (7 3) (5 R)
	V - (3 7) (R 5)
	I - (7/6 3) (5 R)

Rootless Pattern B:

	ii - (3 7) (1 5)
	V - (7 3) (5 1)
	I - (3 7/6) (R 5)

# Substitutions 

The following substitions are common in the right hand of the previous voicings.

ii - in RH, sub R for 9. sub 5 for 11 or 13.
V - in RH, sub R for 9, b9, or #9. sub 5 for #11, 13, or b5.
I - in RH, sub R for 9, 5 for #11, or 13.

#### Examples with extensions/alterations

Pattern A with extensions:

	ii - (R 7) (3 5 9)
	V - (R 3) (7 b9 13)
	I - (R 7/6) (3 5 9)

Pattern B with extensions:

	ii - (R 3) (7 1 11)
	V - (R 7) (3 #5 b9)
	I - (R 3) (7/6 9 5)

Rootless Pattern A:

	ii - (7 3) (5 9)
	V - (3 7) (9 13)
	I - (7/6 3) (13 9) 

{note, LH should play 7, not 6, if RH is playing 13)

Rootless Pattern B:

	ii - (3 7) (9 5)
	V - (7 3) (5 9)
	I - (3 7/6) (9 #11)
